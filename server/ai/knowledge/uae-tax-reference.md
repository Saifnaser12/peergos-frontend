# UAE Tax Reference

## Corporate Income Tax (CIT)
- 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000; 9% above (Federal Decree-Law 47/2022).
- Small Business Relief: resident businesses with revenue ≤ AED 3,000,000 may
  elect no taxable income — available for tax periods ending on or before
  31 December 2026 only.
- Qualifying Free Zone Persons: 0% on qualifying income, strict conditions.
- CIT return + payment due within 9 months of the end of the tax period.
- All taxable persons must register with the FTA even if below AED 375,000.

## VAT
- Standard rate 5% (Federal Decree-Law 8/2017, since 1 Jan 2018).
- Mandatory registration: AED 375,000 taxable supplies (previous 12 months
  or expected next 30 days). Voluntary: AED 187,500.
- Returns usually quarterly via EmaraTax, due within 28 days of period end.
- TRN is a 15-digit number; tax invoices must show supplier TRN, date,
  sequential number, line items, VAT rate/amount, total.

## E-Invoicing
- Peppol-based 5-corner model. July 2026: pilot begins. Phased mandate —
  all businesses in scope by July 2027, via Accredited Service Providers.

## Deadlines summary
- CIT: 9 months after financial year end.
- VAT: 28 days after each period.
- Small Business Relief ends for periods ending after 31 Dec 2026.
